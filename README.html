National Days

Political & Statehood Days

  • January 1: Restoration Day of the Independent Czech State (Commemorates the peaceful split of Czechoslovakia in 1993)
  • May 8: Victory Day (Marks the end of World War II in Europe in 1945)
  • September 28: Czech Statehood Day (St. Wenceslas Day, honoring the patron saint of the nation)
  • October 28: Independent Czechoslovak State Day (Celebrating the original foundation of Czechoslovakia in 1918)
  • November 17: Struggle for Freedom and Democracy Day (Commemorating the student protests of 1939 and the Velvet Revolution of 1989)

Cultural & Religious Days

  • Variable Friday: Good Friday
  • Variable Monday: Easter Monday
  • May 1: Labour Day / May Day (International workers' day and the traditional festival of love)
  • July 5: Saints Cyril and Methodius Day (Honoring the missionaries who brought the Slavic alphabet in 863 AD)
  • July 6: Jan Hus Day (Commemorating the martyrdom of the religious reformer in 1415)
  • December 24: Christmas Eve
  • December 25: Christmas Day
  • December 26: St. Stephen's Day (Second Day of Christmas)
